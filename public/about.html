About FlightxTrack

Live air traffic over India, built on public data.

How it works

  • Aircraft with ADS-B transponders broadcast their position, altitude and speed.
  • Volunteer receivers feed this to community networks, adsb.lol and the OpenSky Network, which share it freely.
  • Our server asks both for the area around India about once a minute, merges the results, caches them, and records counts over time.
  • This website reads the cached data and shows it on the map, in the table and in the charts.

Pages

  • Live Map: every aircraft on a map; click one to see details and its recent trail.
  • Flights: sortable, filterable table of all aircraft.
  • Analytics: airline share, altitude and speed distributions, countries, records and traffic over time.

Limitations

  • Coverage depends on volunteer receivers, so some flights (especially low or remote) may be missing.
  • Airline names are derived from the callsign prefix; unknown prefixes show as unidentified.
  • Origin/destination routes are not part of this data source.
  • This is for information only and must not be used for navigation or safety decisions.
